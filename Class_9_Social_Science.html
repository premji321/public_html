How to Study Social Science

Follow a simple routine to make chapter revision easier.

01. Understand the Chapter

Read the chapter carefully and identify its main ideas, events, terms and examples.

02. Prepare Short Notes

Write headings, subheadings, definitions and important points in your own words.

03. Test Yourself

Answer questions without looking at the book, then revise the topics where you made mistakes.
