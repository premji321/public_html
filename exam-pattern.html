BPSC Exam Pattern 2026

72nd Combined Competitive Examination (CCE)

1 Introduction

The Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) conducts the Combined Competitive Examination (CCE) to recruit candidates for various administrative posts in Bihar.

The selection process consists of three stages:

Preliminary Examination
→
Main Examination
→
Interview
📝

Preliminary Examination

Tests general knowledge through objective-type questions.

📚

Main Examination

Evaluates subject knowledge, analytical ability and written expression.

🎯

Interview

Assesses personality, communication skills and administrative suitability.

Important: The latest official notification should be treated as the final authority for the examination pattern, marks and qualifying requirements.

2 BPSC Exam Structure

Stage Type of Examination Duration Marks
Preliminary Examination Objective (MCQs) 2 Hours 150
Main Examination Descriptive + Qualifying Objective Paper 3 Hours per descriptive paper; 2 Hours for the optional paper 1,100
Interview Personality Test As prescribed by BPSC 120

3 Preliminary Examination (Prelims)

Particulars Details
Examination Type Objective (MCQs)
Question Paper General Studies
Total Questions 150
Maximum Marks 150
Duration 2 Hours
Correct Answer +1 Mark
Incorrect Answer −1/3 Mark
Nature Qualifying / Screening
Marks Included in Final Merit No
Question Paper Language Hindi and English
Purpose: The Preliminary Examination is conducted to shortlist candidates for the Main Examination.

4 Main Examination (Mains)

The Main Examination evaluates candidates' knowledge, analytical ability and written expression through descriptive papers and applicable qualifying papers.

Paper Maximum Marks Duration Nature
General Hindi 100 3 Hours Qualifying
General Studies Paper I 300 3 Hours Merit-based
General Studies Paper II 300 3 Hours Merit-based
Essay 300 3 Hours Merit-based
Optional Subject 100 2 Hours As prescribed in the notification
Total 1,100 — —
Purpose: The Main Examination plays a major role in determining candidates' merit for the Interview stage.

5 Interview (Personality Test)

The Interview is the final stage of the BPSC selection process. It assesses a candidate's personality and suitability for administrative responsibilities.

Particulars Details
Examination Type Personality Test
Maximum Marks 120
Duration As prescribed by BPSC
Eligible Candidates Those qualifying in the Main Examination
Nature Assessment of personality and administrative suitability

Key Assessment Areas

  • Personality and confidence
  • Communication skills
  • Decision-making ability
  • General awareness
  • Understanding of Bihar's social, economic and political issues
  • Administrative aptitude and judgment
Purpose: To assess whether candidates possess the qualities required for administrative responsibilities.

6 Final Merit and Selection

The final selection is made according to the merit calculation and eligibility rules prescribed in the applicable BPSC notification.

Stage Role in Selection
Preliminary Examination Screening for the Main Examination
Main Examination Evaluation of written performance
Interview Assessment of personality and suitability
Final Merit List Prepared according to the notified selection rules
