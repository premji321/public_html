BIHAR PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION

BPSC Examination Syllabus

Subject-wise syllabus for systematic examination preparation. Select a topic from the left menu to navigate directly to that section.

Important: BPSC conducts different examinations for different posts. The syllabus below is a general study outline, not a verified, universal syllabus for every BPSC recruitment. Refer to the official notification for the examination you are preparing for.

Preliminary Examination

General Studies preparation areas commonly studied by BPSC aspirants.

  1. History of India and the Indian National Movement.
  2. Geography of India and the world, with emphasis on Bihar where prescribed.
  3. Indian Polity and Constitution.
  4. Indian Economy and economic development.
  5. General Science and everyday scientific knowledge.
  6. Important national and international current affairs.
  7. General knowledge of Bihar, including its history, geography and culture.
  8. Environment and other topics specified in the relevant official syllabus.

1. History of India

Ancient India

  • Prehistoric cultures and the Indus Valley Civilisation.
  • Vedic period and the rise of Mahajanapadas.
  • Buddhism, Jainism and other religious movements.
  • Rise of Magadha, Mauryan Empire and Ashoka.
  • Gupta Empire and post-Gupta developments.
  • Ancient Indian art, architecture, literature and education.

Medieval India

  • Early medieval kingdoms and regional powers.
  • Delhi Sultanate: major dynasties and administration.
  • Mughal Empire: rulers, administration and culture.
  • Bhakti and Sufi movements.
  • Marathas and other regional powers.

Modern India

  • Arrival of European trading companies.
  • Expansion of British power in India.
  • Governor-Generals and major administrative changes.
  • Revolt of 1857: causes, leaders and consequences.
  • Socio-religious reform movements.
  • Formation of the Indian National Congress.
  • National Movement, Gandhian movements and revolutionary activities.
  • Independence, Partition and constitutional developments.

2. Geography

Physical Geography

  • Earth's structure, rocks and landforms.
  • Mountains, plateaus, plains and drainage systems.
  • Atmosphere, climate, winds and rainfall.
  • Oceans, ocean currents and natural vegetation.

Geography of India

  • Physiographic divisions of India.
  • Major rivers and drainage systems.
  • Climate, monsoon and soil types.
  • Agriculture, minerals, industries and transport.
  • Population distribution and urbanisation.

Geography of Bihar

  • Geographical location and physical divisions.
  • Major rivers, tributaries and flood-prone regions.
  • Climate, soil, agriculture and irrigation.
  • Natural resources and population distribution.

3. Indian Polity and Constitution

  • Historical background and making of the Constitution.
  • Preamble and salient features of the Constitution.
  • Fundamental Rights, Duties and Directive Principles.
  • President, Vice-President and Prime Minister.
  • Parliament and the legislative process.
  • Supreme Court, High Courts and judicial review.
  • Centre-State relations and federalism.
  • Governor, Chief Minister and State Legislature.
  • Local government, Panchayati Raj and municipalities.
  • Constitutional bodies, elections and public administration.

4. Indian Economy

  • Basic concepts of economics.
  • National income and economic growth.
  • Planning and economic development in India.
  • Inflation, unemployment and poverty.
  • Banking system and monetary policy.
  • Fiscal policy, taxation and the Union Budget.
  • Agriculture, industry and the services sector.
  • Government welfare schemes and development programmes.
  • Bihar's economy, agriculture and development challenges.

5. General Science

Physics

  • Motion, force, work, energy and power.
  • Heat, light, sound and electricity.
  • Magnetism and common scientific instruments.

Chemistry

  • Elements, compounds and mixtures.
  • Atoms, molecules, acids, bases and salts.
  • Metals, non-metals and common chemical reactions.

Biology

  • Cell structure and basic life processes.
  • Human body systems, nutrition and diseases.
  • Plants, animals, heredity and biodiversity.

6. Current Affairs

  • Important national and international developments.
  • Government policies and schemes.
  • Reports, indices and major publications.
  • Important appointments, awards and honours.
  • Sports, science and technology developments.
  • Important events related to Bihar.

7. Bihar Special

  • Ancient history of Bihar and Magadha.
  • Bihar's role in the Indian freedom struggle.
  • Important historical places and personalities.
  • Geography, rivers, climate and natural resources.
  • Agriculture, industries and the state economy.
  • Culture, languages, festivals, art and heritage.
  • Administrative structure and development programmes.

8. Environment and Ecology

  • Ecosystems and ecological relationships.
  • Biodiversity and conservation.
  • Climate change and global warming.
  • Air, water and soil pollution.
  • National parks, wildlife sanctuaries and protected areas.
  • Renewable energy and sustainable development.
  • Environmental laws and relevant national initiatives.

Main Examination

Main examination papers vary according to the particular BPSC recruitment. The following is a broad preparation outline, not a universal paper structure.

Language Paper

  • Grammar and language usage.
  • Comprehension and vocabulary.
  • Precis, composition or other prescribed components.

General Studies

  • Indian history and the freedom movement.
  • Geography of India and relevant state topics.
  • Indian Constitution, polity and governance.
  • Economy, development and social issues.
  • Science, technology and current affairs.

Answer Writing

  • Clear introductions and structured answers.
  • Use of relevant facts, examples and explanations.
  • Balanced conclusions and time management.
The exact papers, marks, qualifying rules and topics must be confirmed from the official notification for the relevant BPSC examination.
