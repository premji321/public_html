                  Chapter Title

                  BIHAR PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION

                  BPSC Examination Syllabus

                  Subject-wise syllabus for systematic BPSC examination preparation.

                  Important: The syllabus below is a general preparation outline. Always verify the exact syllabus and examination scheme in the official notification for your target BPSC examination.

                  Syllabus Overview

                  Major areas commonly studied for BPSC General Studies.

                  • Indian History and the History of Bihar
                  • Geography of India and Bihar
                  • Indian Constitution and Polity
                  • Indian Economy and the Economy of Bihar
                  • General Science and Technology
                  • Current Affairs of national and international importance
                  • Environment and Ecology
                  • General Mental Ability, where applicable

                  Preliminary Examination Syllabus

                  Important areas for General Studies preparation.

                  1. General Studies

                  • General Science
                  • Current events of national and international importance
                  • History of India and Bihar
                  • Geography of India and Bihar
                  • Indian Polity and Economy
                  • Indian National Movement
                  • General Mental Ability

                  2. Bihar-Specific Topics

                  • History and culture of Bihar
                  • Geography and rivers of Bihar
                  • Economy and agriculture of Bihar
                  • Important places and personalities
                  • Current affairs related to Bihar

                  History

                  Ancient, medieval and modern Indian history.

                  Ancient Indian History

                  • Prehistoric cultures
                  • Indus Valley Civilization
                  • Vedic Period
                  • Mahajanapadas and the rise of Magadha
                  • Buddhism and Jainism
                  • Mauryan Empire
                  • Gupta Empire

                  Medieval Indian History

                  • Delhi Sultanate
                  • Mughal Empire
                  • Vijayanagara Empire
                  • Bhakti and Sufi Movements
                  • Medieval administration and society

                  Modern Indian History

                  • Advent of Europeans
                  • Battle of Plassey and Battle of Buxar
                  • Expansion of British rule
                  • Revolt of 1857
                  • Socio-religious reform movements
                  • Indian National Congress
                  • Swadeshi Movement
                  • Non-Cooperation Movement
                  • Civil Disobedience Movement
                  • Quit India Movement
                  • Independence and Partition

                  Geography

                  Physical Geography

                  • Earth and its structure
                  • Latitude and longitude
                  • Rocks and minerals
                  • Earthquakes and volcanoes
                  • Atmosphere and climate
                  • Ocean currents

                  Indian Geography

                  • Physiographic divisions of India
                  • Himalayan mountain system
                  • Indian drainage system
                  • Indian monsoon
                  • Soils and natural vegetation
                  • Agriculture and mineral resources
                  • Population and urbanisation

                  Geography of Bihar

                  • Location and boundaries
                  • Rivers and drainage
                  • Climate and soils
                  • Agriculture and irrigation
                  • Floods and droughts
                  • Minerals and industries

                  Indian Polity

                  • Constituent Assembly and making of the Constitution
                  • Salient features of the Constitution
                  • Preamble
                  • Fundamental Rights
                  • Directive Principles of State Policy
                  • Fundamental Duties
                  • President, Vice-President and Prime Minister
                  • Parliament and legislative procedure
                  • Supreme Court and High Courts
                  • Centre-state relations
                  • Constitutional and statutory bodies
                  • Panchayati Raj and local government
                  • Bihar's political and administrative structure

                  Indian Economy

                  • Basic economic concepts
                  • GDP and national income
                  • Inflation and unemployment
                  • Banking and monetary policy
                  • Fiscal policy and taxation
                  • Union Budget
                  • Poverty and inclusive growth
                  • Agriculture and rural development
                  • Economic reforms
                  • Bihar's economy and development indicators

                  General Science

                  Physics

                  • Motion, force and laws of motion
                  • Work, energy and power
                  • Heat and temperature
                  • Light and sound
                  • Electricity and magnetism

                  Chemistry

                  • Atoms and molecules
                  • Elements and compounds
                  • Acids, bases and salts
                  • Metals and non-metals
                  • Everyday chemistry

                  Biology

                  • Cell structure
                  • Human body systems
                  • Nutrition and diseases
                  • Plant and animal biology
                  • Genetics and evolution

                  Current Affairs

                  • National and international events
                  • Government schemes and policies
                  • Reports, rankings and indices
                  • Important appointments
                  • Awards and honours
                  • Sports and important events
                  • Science and technology developments
                  • Bihar-specific current affairs

                  Bihar Special

                  • Ancient history of Bihar and Magadha
                  • Buddhism and Jainism in Bihar
                  • Bihar's role in the freedom movement
                  • Important personalities of Bihar
                  • Geography and rivers of Bihar
                  • Agriculture and economy of Bihar
                  • Culture, festivals and heritage
                  • State government schemes

                  Environment and Ecology

                  • Ecology and ecosystem
                  • Food chains and food webs
                  • Biodiversity and conservation
                  • National parks and wildlife sanctuaries
                  • Pollution and its control
                  • Climate change and global warming
                  • Environmental laws and conventions
                  • Disaster management
