    BIHAR PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION

    BPSC Examination Syllabus

    Subject-wise syllabus, chapter-wise notes and systematic preparation for BPSC examinations.

    Important: This is a general preparation outline. The exact syllabus, examination pattern and qualifying requirements may differ by recruitment. Always check the official notification for your target BPSC examination.

    Syllabus Overview

    • Indian History and Bihar History
    • Geography of India and Bihar
    • Indian Constitution and Polity
    • Indian Economy and Bihar Economy
    • General Science and Technology
    • Current Affairs
    • Environment and Ecology
    • General Mental Ability, where applicable

    Preliminary Examination

    Prepare the subjects prescribed in the official notification. Common General Studies preparation areas include history, geography, polity, economy, science, current affairs and Bihar-specific topics.

    Main Examination

    Prepare the prescribed General Studies and other papers according to the applicable BPSC notification. Practise answer writing and previous-year questions.

    How to Use Subject-wise Notes

    1. Click Subject-wise Notes in the left sidebar.
    2. Select a subject, such as History.
    3. Select a subtopic, such as Modern History.
    4. Choose the chapter you want to study.
    5. The selected chapter's notes will appear here.
