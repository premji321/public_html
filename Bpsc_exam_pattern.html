Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) – Exam Pattern 2026

72nd Combined Competitive Examination (CCE)

1. Introduction

The Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) conducts the Combined Competitive Examination (CCE) to recruit candidates for various administrative posts in Bihar. The selection process consists of three stages: Preliminary Examination, Main Examination and Interview.

1. Preliminary Examination Prelims
2. Main Examination Mains
3. Interview Personality Test
Important: The latest official notification should be treated as the final authority for the examination pattern, marks, and qualifying requirements.

2. BPSC Exam Structure

Stage Type of Examination Duration Marks
Preliminary Examination Objective (MCQs) 2 Hours 150
Main Examination Descriptive + Qualifying Objective Paper 3 Hours per descriptive paper; 2 Hours for optional paper 1,100
Interview Personality Test As prescribed by BPSC 120

3. Preliminary Examination (Prelims)

Particulars Details
Examination Type Objective (MCQs)
Total Questions 150
Maximum Marks 150
Duration 2 Hours
Correct Answer +1 Mark
Incorrect Answer −1/3 Mark
Nature Qualifying
Marks Included in Final Merit No

Revised Answer Format: Option E (Not Attempted) has been introduced, according to the supplied reference. Candidates should follow the latest official instructions regarding unanswered questions and negative marking.

4. Main Examination (Mains)

Paper Subject Marks Duration Nature
Paper I General Hindi 100 3 Hours Qualifying
Paper II General Studies Paper I 300 3 Hours Merit
Paper III General Studies Paper II 300 3 Hours Merit
Paper IV Essay 300 3 Hours Merit
Paper V Optional Subject 100 2 Hours Qualifying
Total 1,100

5. Qualifying Marks

Category Minimum Qualifying Marks
General Hindi 30% (as stated in the supplied reference)
General Category — Optional Paper 40%
Backward Classes (BC) 36.5%
Extremely Backward Classes (EBC) 34%
SC/ST, Women and Persons with Disabilities 32%
The qualifying requirements should be checked against the applicable official notification.

6. Interview (Personality Test)

Particulars Details
Maximum Marks 120
Nature Personality Test
Purpose Assessment of personality and administrative suitability
Candidates Called According to the commission's selection rules

7. Final Merit Calculation

Component Marks
General Studies Paper I 300
General Studies Paper II 300
Essay 300
Interview 120
Total Merit Marks 1,020

General Hindi, the qualifying optional paper and Prelims marks are excluded from the merit calculation under the structure described in the supplied reference.

8. Selection Process

Prelims Screening examination
Mains Written examination and qualifying papers
Interview Personality assessment

Selection sequence: Prelims → Mains → Interview → Final Merit List

  • Prelims: Screening examination.
  • Mains: Written examination and qualifying papers.
  • Interview: Personality assessment.
  • Final Selection: Based on merit and the applicable BPSC rules.
