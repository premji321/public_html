BPSC Exam Pattern 2026

72nd BPSC Combined Competitive Examination (CCE)

The Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) conducts the Combined Competitive Examination (CCE) to recruit candidates for various administrative and other government positions in Bihar.

The selection process generally consists of three stages:

1. Preliminary Examination
(Prelims)
2. Main Examination
(Mains)
3. Interview
(Personality Test)
Important: The latest official notification should be treated as the final authority for the examination pattern, marks, and qualifying requirements.

1. BPSC Prelims Exam Pattern

Particulars Details
Examination Preliminary Examination
Type of Questions Objective (MCQs)

Total Marks 150

Subject General Studies
Negative Marking 1/3 mark for each incorrect answer, as specified in the notification
Purpose Screening candidates for the Mains Examination

2. Minimum Qualifying Marks

The following qualifying percentages have been used in BPSC examinations:

Category Minimum Qualifying Percentage
General / Unreserved 40%
Backward Classes (BC) 36.5%
Extremely Backward Classes (EBC) 34%
Scheduled Castes (SC) 32%
Scheduled Tribes (ST) 32%
Women 32%
Note: These are commonly prescribed minimum qualifying percentages. Check the applicable notification for the specific examination and category. The actual cutoff may be higher.

Total 1,100 As shown in this commonly cited structure

5. How Is the Final Merit List Prepared?

The general selection process is:

Prelims
Mains
Interview
Final Merit List
  • Prelims: Screens candidates for the Mains Examination.
  • Mains: Tests subject knowledge, analytical ability and written expression.
  • Interview: Assesses personality and suitability for administrative responsibilities.
  • Final Merit List: Prepared according to the marks and rules specified in the official notification.

6. Preparation Strategy for BPSC Prelims

For effective preparation, divide your study time among the following areas:

Priority Subject Preparation Focus
High Modern Indian History British expansion, Governor-Generals, Revolt of 1857 and National Movement
High Indian Polity Constitution, Parliament, Fundamental Rights and constitutional bodies
High Geography Indian physiography, rivers, climate and Bihar geography
High General Science Biology, Physics, Chemistry and everyday science
High Current Affairs Bihar, national and international developments
Medium Ancient and Medieval History Mauryas, Guptas, Delhi Sultanate and Mughals
Medium Economy Basic concepts, inflation, banking and government schemes

Recommended Practice Plan

  1. Study important topics based on previous-year questions.
  2. Revise factual information regularly.
  3. Solve topic-wise MCQs after completing each chapter.
  4. Attempt full-length mock tests under timed conditions.
  5. Analyse incorrect answers and revise weak areas.
