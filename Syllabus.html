2. Simplified Detailed Syllabus

A. History of India and Bihar

Ancient India

Indus Valley Civilization, Vedic Age, Jainism, Buddhism, Mauryan Empire and Gupta Empire.

Medieval India

Delhi Sultanate, Mughal Empire, Bhakti and Sufi movements.

Modern India

British expansion, Revolt of 1857, socio-religious reform movements and the Indian National Movement.

History of Bihar

Ancient Magadha, Nalanda, Santhal Uprising, Kunwar Singh, Champaran Satyagraha and Bihar's contribution to the freedom struggle.

B. Geography of India and Bihar

  • Physical features: mountains, plateaus and plains.
  • Rivers, drainage systems and the Indian monsoon.
  • Climate, soils and natural vegetation.
  • Agriculture, irrigation, minerals and industries.
  • Population, migration and urbanisation.
  • Bihar's rivers, flood-prone regions, agricultural patterns and natural resources.

C. Indian Polity

  • Constitution, Preamble and salient features.
  • Fundamental Rights, Directive Principles and Fundamental Duties.
  • President, Prime Minister and Parliament.
  • Governor, Chief Minister and State Legislature.
  • Supreme Court, High Courts and judicial review.
  • Constitutional bodies, elections and Panchayati Raj.

D. Indian Economy and Bihar Economy

  • GDP, inflation, poverty and unemployment.
  • Economic planning, Five-Year Plans and NITI Aayog.
  • Agriculture, industries, banking and government budgets.
  • Economic development, inclusive growth and infrastructure.
  • Bihar's economy, agriculture, industrial development, migration and employment.
  • Bihar Budget, Economic Survey and government development schemes.

E. General Science and Technology

  • Basic school-level Physics, Chemistry and Biology.
  • Human body, nutrition, diseases and genetics.
  • Space technology, computers, biotechnology and artificial intelligence.
  • Environment, biodiversity, climate change and renewable energy.

F. Current Affairs

  • National and international events.
  • Government policies and schemes.
  • Awards, appointments, reports and important developments.
  • Science and technology, environment and international relations.
  • Bihar government initiatives, budget and important state developments.

G. General Mental Ability

  • Number series, analogy and classification.
  • Coding-decoding and logical reasoning.
  • Basic numerical reasoning.
  • Simple data interpretation.

Marks Distribution

Section Subject Marks

B Current Events of National and International Importance 114

Total 300

A. Modern History of India and Indian Culture — 114 Marks

1. Modern Indian History

  • British expansion and establishment of colonial rule.
  • British administrative and economic policies.
  • Revolt of 1857.
  • Socio-religious reform movements.
  • Rise of Indian nationalism.
  • Formation and development of the Indian National Congress.
  • Swadeshi and revolutionary movements.
  • Gandhian movements and the freedom struggle.
  • Constitutional developments and Independence.

2. Bihar's Contribution to the Freedom Struggle

  • Santhal Uprising (1855–56).
  • Revolt of 1857 and Kunwar Singh.
  • Birsa Munda and tribal resistance.
  • Champaran Satyagraha (1917).
  • Non-Cooperation and Civil Disobedience movements.
  • Quit India Movement in Bihar.
  • Role of Dr. Rajendra Prasad, Jayaprakash Narayan and other important leaders.

3. Western Education in Bihar

  • Introduction of Western education under British rule.
  • Development of schools, colleges and universities.
  • Expansion of technical and professional education.
  • Rise of the educated middle class.
  • Impact of education on political awareness and social reform.

4. Indian Art and Culture

  • Mauryan art and architecture.
  • Gupta art and culture.
  • Pala art and Buddhist heritage.
  • Patna Kalam painting.
  • Nalanda and Vikramashila.
  • Important cultural traditions and heritage sites of Bihar.

5. Important Personalities

Mahatma Gandhi, Rabindranath Tagore, Jawaharlal Nehru, Dr. Rajendra Prasad and other important personalities associated with modern India and Bihar.

B. Current Events — 114 Marks

National Current Affairs

  • Government policies, schemes and legislation.
  • Important constitutional and legal developments.
  • Economy, budget and major reports.
  • Education, healthcare and social issues.
  • Science and technology.
  • Environment and climate change.
  • Awards, appointments and important national events.

International Current Affairs

  • International organisations.
  • India's foreign relations.
  • International summits and agreements.
  • Major global political and economic developments.
  • International reports and important events.

Bihar Current Affairs

  • Bihar government schemes.
  • Bihar Budget and Economic Survey.
  • Infrastructure and development projects.
  • Education, healthcare and agricultural initiatives.
  • Important appointments, awards and state developments.

C. Statistical Analysis, Graphs and Diagrams — 72 Marks

  • Interpretation of tables and statistical data.
  • Bar graphs, line graphs and pie charts.
  • Percentages, ratios and growth rates.
  • Comparison of datasets.
  • Identification of trends and patterns.
  • Drawing conclusions from data.
  • Identifying errors, limitations and inconsistencies.
Preparation focus: Practise data interpretation regularly and learn to draw accurate conclusions from statistical information.

4. ESSAY PAPER — 300 MARKS

The Essay paper should be prepared separately because it tests analytical thinking, structured writing and the ability to present balanced arguments.

A. Moral and Philosophical Essays

Truth, honesty and integrity; ethics in public life; discipline and responsibility; knowledge, wisdom and education; justice and equality; leadership and human values.

B. Hypothetical Essays

Ethical dilemmas in administration; decision-making during a crisis; balancing public interest and individual rights; disaster management; resolving social conflicts; balancing development and environmental protection.

C. Social, Economic, Political, Religious and Administrative Issues

Social Issues

Education and literacy, women empowerment, poverty and inequality, youth and employment, social justice and harmony.

Economic Issues

Economic development, agriculture and rural development, unemployment and migration, industrialisation, inclusive growth.

Political and Administrative Issues

Democracy and constitutional values, good governance, transparency and accountability, corruption and administrative reforms, citizen participation.

Religious and Cultural Issues

Secularism, religious harmony, cultural diversity and preservation of heritage.

D. Self-Reliant India and Digital India

  • Atmanirbhar Bharat.
  • Domestic manufacturing.
  • Entrepreneurship and employment.
  • Digital governance.
  • Digital literacy and financial inclusion.
  • Technology and rural development.

E. Problems, Solutions and Possibilities of Bihar

Problems

  • Poverty and unemployment.
  • Migration.
  • Floods and agricultural challenges.
  • Industrial underdevelopment.
  • Education and healthcare gaps.

Solutions and Possibilities

  • Agricultural modernisation.
  • Industrial development.
  • Skill development.
  • Infrastructure expansion.
  • Tourism and cultural heritage.
  • Digital governance.
  • Employment generation and human development.

F. Popular Proverbs and Sayings of Bihar

  • Bhojpuri proverbs.
  • Maithili sayings.
  • Magahi sayings.
  • Folk wisdom related to morality, agriculture, education and social life.
  • Meaning, interpretation and contemporary relevance of popular sayings.

Essay Writing Structure

  1. Introduction.
  2. Background and context.
  3. Main arguments.
  4. Relevant examples and facts.
  5. Problems and challenges.
  6. Solutions and way forward.
  7. Balanced conclusion.
