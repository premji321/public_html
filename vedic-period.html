Overview of the major preparation areas for the Combined Competitive Examination.

Preliminary Examination

General Studies, history, geography, polity, economy, science and current affairs.

📚

Language, General Studies papers and the applicable optional or other paper structure.

🎤

Interview

Major General Studies Topics

  • Indian History and the National Movement
  • Ancient, Medieval and Modern India
  • Geography of India and Bihar
  • Indian Constitution and Political System
  • Indian Economy and Bihar Economy
  • General Science and Technology
  • Environment and Ecology
  • Current Affairs: National and International
  • Bihar's History, Culture and Society
  • Logical reasoning and aptitude, where applicable to the examination

Note: This is a preparation overview, not a verified notification-specific syllabus. Check the official notification for the exact examination year, paper structure, qualifying rules and marks.

Understand the stages of the Combined Competitive Examination.

Stage General Format Purpose
Preliminary Objective questions Screening examination
Main Examination Written/descriptive papers as notified Assessment of knowledge and analytical ability
Interview Personality assessment Suitability for the relevant posts

Important

Exact marks, duration, qualifying language papers, negative marking, merit calculation and interview weightage can vary according to the applicable notification. Verify these details before using this section as an exam reference.

Cutoff Analysis

Cutoffs depend on the examination cycle, category, vacancies, paper difficulty and applicable selection rules.

How to analyse a cutoff

  • Record the examination year and notification number.
  • Record category-wise official cutoff marks.
  • Compare the same examination stage across years.
  • Consider vacancies and paper difficulty.
  • Use historical cutoffs as guidance, not a guarantee.
