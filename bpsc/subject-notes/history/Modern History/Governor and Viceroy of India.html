British Administration in India (1773–1950)

British rule in India was administered through Governors-General and Viceroys. The administration changed from East India Company rule to Crown rule in 1858 and finally to independent India.

1. Governor-General vs Viceroy

BASIC CONCEPT
Governor-GeneralViceroy
Initially represented East India Company.Represented British Crown.
Position developed under Parliamentary Acts.Position established after 1857 Revolt.
Administered British Indian territories.Administered India on behalf of British monarch.
First: Warren Hastings (Bengal, 1773).First: Lord Canning (1858).
Important: Lord Canning was the last Governor-General under Company rule and the first Viceroy under Crown rule.
