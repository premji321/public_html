1. BPSC Examination Overview

The Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) conducts the Combined Competitive Examination (CCE) to recruit candidates for various administrative and other government posts in Bihar.

Three Stages of Examination

  1. Preliminary Examination (Prelims)
  2. Main Examination (Mains)
  3. Interview (Personality Test)

2. Preliminary Examination (Prelims)

Objective Type – MCQ
ParticularsDetails
Total Marks150
Total Questions150
Duration2 Hours
Question TypeMultiple Choice Questions
Negative Marking1/3 mark for each incorrect answer under the revised scheme
NatureQualifying / Screening

27. BPSC Examination Pattern at a Glance

Stage / Paper Marks Nature
Prelims – General Studies 150 Objective
General Hindi 100 Qualifying
GS Paper I 300 Descriptive
GS Paper II 300 Descriptive
Essay 300 Descriptive
Optional Subject 100 Qualifying for general posts
Interview 120 Personality Test
Important: This is a reference overview of the revised scheme. The applicable 72nd CCE advertisement and corrigenda must be checked for the final examination pattern. Specialised posts may have different requirements.

Important Points

  • Prelims duration: 2 hours.
  • Prelims has negative marking of 1/3 mark for an incorrect answer under the revised scheme.
  • The fifth option E is used for questions not attempted under the revised instructions.
  • Prelims marks are not included in final merit.
  • General Hindi requires at least 30% marks.
  • Final merit for general posts is based on the prescribed Mains merit papers and Interview.
