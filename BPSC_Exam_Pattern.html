BIHAR PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION

BPSC Exam Pattern

Understand the examination stages, paper structure, marks, duration and selection process.

Important notice: BPSC conducts different examinations for different posts. This page mainly explains the general structure of the Combined Competitive Examination (CCE). Paper names, marks, duration, negative marking, qualifying rules and selection stages can change by advertisement. Always verify the specific notification and latest instructions on the official BPSC website before the examination.

1. Overview of the Selection Process

STAGE 01

Preliminary Exam

Objective-type screening examination, usually consisting of General Studies MCQs.

STAGE 02

Main Exam

Written examination. Paper structure and qualifying/scoring rules depend on the applicable CCE notification.

STAGE 03

Interview

Personality assessment for posts where an interview is prescribed.

Post-specific rules apply Some BPSC recruitments may use a written examination only, an interview, a skill/typing test, or another process rather than all three CCE stages.

The CCE Preliminary Examination is generally an objective screening test. The following table is a quick reference; confirm the details from your examination advertisement.

Common General Studies Areas

  • General Science
  • Current events of national and international importance
  • History of India and the history of Bihar
  • Geography of India and Bihar
  • Indian Polity and Economy
  • Indian National Movement
  • General Mental Ability, where included in the applicable syllabus
Answer-option reminder: BPSC has issued a notice about introducing a fifth answer option, “E”, in MCQ-based examinations. Read the instructions for your specific exam carefully and do not assume that older OMR rules still apply.

The Main Examination is written. The exact papers, their marks, duration, optional-paper rules and qualifying status must be taken from the notification for the relevant CCE cycle.

Preparation strategy

  • Study the official paper-wise syllabus before making a timetable.
  • Practise previous-year questions for each paper.
  • Write timed answers and review structure, accuracy and relevance.
  • Keep separate notes for Bihar-specific history, geography, economy and current affairs.
  • Check which papers are qualifying and which contribute to merit.

4. Interview / Personality Test

For recruitments that include an interview, candidates who qualify the written stages may be called for a personality assessment. The applicable notification defines eligibility, marks, selection criteria and the final merit calculation.

  • Practise clear, balanced and evidence-based answers.
  • Review Bihar's administration, social issues, economy and current affairs.
  • Be prepared to discuss your educational background and relevant experience.
  • Do not assume a fixed interview mark total unless it is stated in your notification.

5. Negative Marking and OMR Instructions

Negative marking is not safe to assume from a generic BPSC pattern page. It may depend on the examination and the latest instructions.

  • Read the advertisement and the latest exam notice carefully.
  • Check the number of answer options, including whether option E is used.
  • Understand how to mark an answer and how unanswered questions are treated.
  • Follow the OMR instructions on the question paper and admit card.

6. Final Merit and Selection

Final selection rules differ by post. The notification specifies which examination stages contribute to merit, whether qualifying papers are excluded from ranking, how interview marks are treated, and how ties or category-wise vacancies are handled.

Before relying on any marks table, check the exact advertisement number and examination cycle relevant to your application.

7. Official BPSC Resources

Use the official Commission website to check the latest advertisements, corrigenda, exam notices, answer-option instructions, examination calendar and results.
