70th BPSC General Studies Paper–I

Time Allowed: 3 Hours Full Marks: 300

Section I — 114 Marks

Question 1. Write short notes on the following:

(a) The Indian Councils Act, 1861. 8 Marks
(b) Role of Bihar in the Quit India Movement. 8 Marks
(c) Santhal Uprising. 8 Marks
(d) Patna Kalam Painting. 7 Marks
(e) Art and Architecture of the Mauryan Age. 7 Marks

Question 2

(a) Describe the Champaran Indigo Movement and explain its impact on the Indian freedom struggle. 38 Marks
OR
(b) To what extent were British policies responsible for the emergence of the Indian National Movement? Explain. 38 Marks

Question 3

(a) Examine the expansion of Western education in Bihar from the mid-nineteenth century onwards. 38 Marks
OR
(b) Assess the constructive works of Mahatma Gandhi. 38 Marks

Section II — 114 Marks

Question 4. Answer the following questions:

(a) Discuss the prospects for unity and coalition politics among opposition parties following the defeat of the Aam Aadmi Party in the 2025 Delhi Assembly elections. 8 Marks
(b) The Russia–Ukraine war has completed three years. Explain the causes of the war and discuss its effects on world politics. 8 Marks
(c) The Maha Kumbh Mela was organised in India from 13 January to 26 February 2025. How did this event symbolise Indian culture, religious faith and social harmony? Explain with examples. 8 Marks
(d) Explain in detail the need for and importance of a caste-based census in Bihar. 7 Marks
(e) Analyse the likely future of the United States at the national and international levels under the leadership of newly elected President Donald Trump. 7 Marks

Question 5

(a) Uttarakhand has become the first state in independent India to implement a Uniform Civil Code. Explain the Uniform Civil Code in this context. Also discuss, with examples, the changes it may bring to Indian society in the social, cultural, religious and legal spheres. 38 Marks
OR
(b) Discuss the constitutional provisions governing President's Rule in India. Explain the reasons for the recent imposition of President's Rule in Manipur and evaluate its effectiveness in contemporary times. 38 Marks

Question 6

(a) Discuss the concept of "One Nation, One Election" and its implications for India's federal structure. What are the potential advantages and disadvantages of synchronising elections to the Lok Sabha and State Legislative Assemblies? Analyse. 38 Marks
OR
(b) Discuss the increasing participation of women in Indian politics and society, with special reference to the Women's Reservation Act, 2023 (Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam). Analyse the potential social, economic and political changes associated with its implementation. 38 Marks

69th BPSC General Studies Paper–I

Time Allowed: 3 Hours Full Marks: 300

Section I — 114 Marks

Question 1. Write short notes on the following:

(a) Indian Councils Act, 1892. 8 Marks
(b) Santhal Uprising. 8 Marks
(c) Champaran Satyagraha. 8 Marks
(d) Art and Architecture of the Mauryan Period. 7 Marks
(e) Cave Paintings of Eastern India in the Ancient Period. 7 Marks

Question 2

(a) "Bihar has been the centre of learning and spiritualism since ancient times." Explain in detail. 38 Marks
OR
(b) Evaluate the Champaran Indigo Movement and explain its impact on the Indian freedom struggle. 38 Marks

Question 3

(a) Critically examine the contribution of the Moderate Phase of the Indian National Congress Movement. 38 Marks
OR
(b) Trace the development of modern education in Bihar and its impact. 38 Marks

Section II — 114 Marks

Question 4. Answer the following questions:

(a) Explain the concerns of the Supreme Court of India regarding the elimination of gender stereotypes. 8 Marks
(b) Examine the relevance of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023, to the Indian system of governance. 8 Marks
(c) Evaluate the role of Artificial Intelligence in the decision-making processes of legislative institutions. 8 Marks
(d) Describe the parameters and impacts of the Global Wage Report, 2022–23. 7 Marks
(e) What are the key provisions of the Foreign Trade Policy, 2023, regarding export promotion initiatives in India? 7 Marks

Question 5

(a) Provide a brief overview of the Manipur crisis. In your view, what could be the potential repercussions of this ethno-religious conflict for the landscape of national politics? Comment. 38 Marks
OR
(b) How does India's leadership within the G20 reflect its commitment to upholding the ancient philosophy of "Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam" ("The World Is One Family")? Examine how India's approach aligns with this philosophy and contributes to global cooperation and development through its role in the G20. 38 Marks

Question 6

(a) What could be the potential positive and negative societal consequences of introducing a Uniform Civil Code in India, considering its rich diversity? Explain. 38 Marks
OR
(b) How does India's growing status as an emerging space power align with its ambitions and achievements in space exploration, particularly in the context of ISRO's Chandrayaan-3 mission? Discuss the mission's objectives and its role in India's journey towards becoming a prominent player in the global space arena. 38 Marks
